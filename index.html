About

Hello, I'm Pradeeshwar 🌿

I am an ornithologist and wildlife researcher interested in how ecological processes and disturbance regimes shape bird communities across space and time. My work brings together community ecology, macroecology, disturbance ecology, and landscape ecology.

During my M.Sc. in Wildlife Science (Ornithology) at Sálim Ali Centre for Ornithology and Natural History (SACON), I worked on the role of pyrodiversity and burn severity in structuring avifaunal communities in southern tropical dry deciduous forests of Bandipur Tiger Reserve, Karnataka.

I enjoy combining intensive fieldwork (point counts, biodiversity surveys) with quantitative tools, GIS & remote sensing, and statistical modelling to answer applied conservation questions.
